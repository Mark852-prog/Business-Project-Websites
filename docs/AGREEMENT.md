# Website agreement (template)

*Fill in the brackets and send it as a message or PDF. The client replies "I agree" or signs. Claude can translate it. This is a simple template, not legal advice.*

---

**Between:** [Your name], [your address / business registration] ("Designer")
**And:** [Client legal name], trading as [Business name], [address] ("Client")
**Date:** [date]

**1. The website.** The Designer builds a one-page website for the Client that includes services and prices, a booking button or booking request form, opening hours, contact details, a directions link, and a legal notice and privacy page.

**2. Price.** Set-up fee **€[149]**, due before the website goes live. [Optional: care plan **€[15]/month**, paid monthly in advance, cancellable any time with 30 days' notice.]

**3. What the care plan includes.** Hosting, keeping the site online, and up to [2] small changes per month (for example prices, hours, photos or text). Larger changes, like new pages, are quoted separately.

**4. Domain.** The Client buys and owns their domain name. The Designer connects it to the website.

**5. Content.** The Client provides the photos, logo, texts and reviews, and confirms they have the right to use them. The Designer is not responsible for content the Client supplies.

**6. Ownership.** After the set-up fee is paid, the Client may use the website's content and design for their business. If the care plan ends, the Designer hands over the website files on request.

**7. Timeline.** The website goes live within [7] days of receiving the Client's content and payment.

**8. Liability.** The Designer takes reasonable care to keep the website working but is not liable for lost business caused by outages of third-party services (hosting, WhatsApp, booking platforms).
