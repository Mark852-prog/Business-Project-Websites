# Email outreach (gîtes and chambres d'hôtes)

Claude finds the leads and writes each email as a **Gmail draft**. You open Gmail → Drafts and click **Send**. Nothing goes out without you.

## Why this niche
- Owners publish their email on Facebook, tourist office pages and Gîtes de France listings.
- Booking.com and Airbnb take roughly 15–17% of every stay, so one direct booking pays for the website.
- Foreign guests need English (or German or Dutch), which fits multi-language sites.

## How a batch is built
1. Search `site:facebook.com "chambre d'hôtes" <region> "@gmail.com"` (or "gîte").
2. For each result, search the business name and **skip it if it already has its own website**.
3. Note the hosts' first names and one or two specific details (reviews, pool, table d'hôtes, the building's history).
4. Write a personal email, in English if the hosts are English-speaking.
5. Save the batch to `leads/gites-batch-N.md`.

## Rules
- **At most about 20 emails a day** from a new Gmail account, sent by hand, spread across the day.
- Every email names the sender and ends with an opt-out line ("répondez « non merci »"). Anyone who opts out is marked in the leads file and never contacted again.
- Only one follow-up, 5–7 days later.
- The first email offers a **free preview** and doesn't include a link. Claude builds the demo only when someone says yes, so we never publish a site for a business that didn't ask.

## Follow-up template (FR)
> Bonjour,
>
> Je me permets de revenir vers vous au sujet de mon message de la semaine dernière : je serais ravi de vous préparer gratuitement un aperçu de site pour [nom], sans aucun engagement.
>
> Bonne journée,
> Mark
>
> PS : si vous ne souhaitez pas recevoir d'autres messages, répondez simplement « non merci ».
