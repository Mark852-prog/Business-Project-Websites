# Sales kit

## Who to target

The best leads are **appointment-based businesses that take bookings by DM or phone** and have **no website**:

- Barbershops (the best fit: many are small, busy and booked via DMs)
- Nail, lash and brow studios
- Beauty and cosmetic salons, and massage studios
- Tattoo and piercing studios
- Dog groomers
- Personal trainers, physio and yoga studios

**Good signs:** a bio that says "DM to book" or "WhatsApp for appointments", a Linktree with only Instagram, a Google Maps listing with no website, posted price lists, active posting and good reviews.
**Skip:** chains and franchises, businesses that already have a decent website, and inactive accounts.

## Where to find them (free)

1. **Google Maps:** search "barber" or "nail salon" plus a neighbourhood. Open each listing and check whether it has a **Website** button. No button means a lead.
2. **Instagram:** search hashtags like `#barberberlin`, `#nailslyon` or `#barbershopdublin`, and location tags. Check the bio for a link.
3. **Booksy / Fresha / Treatwell:** businesses listed there with no website of their own. For these, the site gets a "Book online" button pointing to their existing booking page.

Start in **your own city and language**. It's easier to trust someone local, and you can visit in person, which converts far better than DMs.

## DM scripts

Always send the demo link, and personalise the first line. Claude can translate any of these into the client's language.

### First message (Instagram DM)

> Hi [Name]! Love your work, especially the [specific cut/nail set from a recent post] 🔥
>
> I noticed you don't have a website yet, so I made a quick preview of what one could look like for [Business]:
> 👉 [demo link]
>
> Customers can see your prices and book straight to your WhatsApp. It's already made, so no pressure. Just tell me if you'd like it live. It'd be €[price] and I handle everything.

### Follow-up (3–4 days later, only once)

> Hey [Name], just checking you saw the site I made for [Business] 🙂 [link]
> Happy to change colours, prices or anything else. If it's not for you, no worries at all!

### When they ask "why do I need a website if I have Instagram?"

> Instagram is great for people who already follow you. A website is how **new** people find you on Google when they search "barber near me", and it lets them book at 11pm without you answering DMs. Most people check a website before trying a new place.

### When they say "it's too expensive"

> Totally understand. It's €[price] once, and if it brings in just [2] new customers it's paid for itself. I can also do [founding offer] because you'd be one of my first clients.

### When they say "I'll think about it"

> Of course! The preview stays up for 2 weeks, so have a look whenever. Any questions, just message me.

### In person (the best way to convert)

Walk in at a quiet time (weekday mornings) and show the demo on your phone:
> "Hi, I'm [name], I make websites for local shops. I made this one for you. Have a look, and if you like it I can put it live this week."

## Legal notes (not legal advice)

- **Invoicing:** to take money regularly you usually need to register as self-employed or a sole trader. Most EU countries have a simple small-business scheme with no VAT under a threshold, for example *Kleinunternehmerregelung* (DE/AT), *micro-entreprise* (FR) or *autónomo* (ES). Check your own country's rules.
- **Cold outreach:** a personal Instagram DM to a business is generally fine. Bulk emails are riskier under EU rules, especially in Germany, so keep it personal and low-volume.
- **Content:** only publish photos, logos and reviews the client has given you or allowed you to use.
