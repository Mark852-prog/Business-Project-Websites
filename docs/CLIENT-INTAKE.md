# Client intake

Send this once a client says yes. Paste their answers to Claude, and Claude fills in the site.

> Great, welcome! 🎉 To finish your site I just need a few things (bullet points or voice notes are fine):
>
> 1. **Services and prices.** Anything to change from the preview?
> 2. **Opening hours**, including lunch breaks and the days you're closed
> 3. **Booking:** should requests go to your **WhatsApp** (number?), or do you use Booksy/Fresha/Treatwell (link?)
> 4. **Phone, email and address** to show on the site
> 5. **5–10 photos** of your work and the shop (just send them here in the best quality you have)
> 6. **Logo**, if you have one
> 7. **2–3 sentences about you/your shop.** How did you start? What makes you different?
> 8. **Can I use some of your Google reviews on the site?** (yes/no)
> 9. **For the legal page:** your full legal name or company name (and VAT/registration number if you have one)
> 10. **Domain:** do you already own one? If not, I'll tell you how to buy `[businessname].[de/fr/ie…]` for about €10/year in your name.
