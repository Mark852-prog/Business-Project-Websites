## Details collected so far (for building their demo sites)

**La Star Barber:** hours Mon–Wed 10:30–18:00, Thu 10:30–19:00, Fri 10:00–12:30 and 14:00–18:00, Sat 10:00–18:00, Sun closed. Services include a classic cut, a specialty cut, and beard care (express, beard, luxury).

**Golden Barber Shop:** open every day, about 9:00–20:00 (listings vary slightly), walk-in. Cut from about €11–13, beard from about €5–8.

**My Barber Shop:** Mon–Sat 9:00–19:00, non-stop. Cuts, beard (blade contouring, threading), facial care.

**Barber H:** Mon–Sat 9:30–19:00, Sun 14:30–19:00.

**Lorelei Da Barber:** men's cuts, beard trimming, traditional shaving. Bookings only through Planity.
